# Story Mode Player Guide 📖

## Welcome to Enhanced Story Mode!

Your journey now includes branching paths, multiple difficulties, cinematic cutscenes, character backstories, and hidden bosses!

---

## 🎮 Getting Started

### Main Story Interface
When you open Story Mode, you'll see:
- **Campaign Map**: All story stages laid out
- **Control Buttons** (top-right):
  - 📖 **Backstories**: View character lore
  - ⚔️ **Difficulty**: Change challenge level
  - 👁️ **Secret Bosses**: View hidden encounters

### Your Progress
- **Green Checkmark**: Stage completed
- **Unlocked**: Stage available to play
- **Locked** 🔒: Complete previous stages first
- **Boss Badge**: Special boss battle

---

## ⚔️ Difficulty Levels

Click the difficulty icon (⚔️) to change challenge:

### 🌱 Novice (Beginner)
- AI plays at 60% strength
- You get +20% bonus
- Perfect for learning

### ⚔️ Warrior (Standard)
- Balanced difficulty
- 1.5x rewards
- Recommended for most players

### 🏆 Master (Hard)
- AI plays 30% stronger
- You get -10% penalty
- 2x rewards
- **Unlock**: Complete Chapter 1

### 👑 Legendary (Ultimate)
- AI plays 50% stronger
- You get -20% penalty
- 3x rewards
- **Unlock**: Complete all chapters on Master

**💡 Tip**: Higher difficulty = Better rewards!

---

## 📖 Story Choices

At certain points, you'll face important decisions:

### How Choices Work
1. Story pauses with a question
2. Multiple choice cards appear
3. Click a choice to see consequences
4. Confirm your decision
5. Your choice affects the story!

### Choice Points
- **Chapter 1**: Light Path vs Dark Path
- **Chapter 2**: Accept Alliance vs Go Solo
- **Chapter 3**: Keep Artifact vs Destroy It
- **Final Battle**: Redemption vs Justice vs Sacrifice

### Choice Effects
- ✨ **Unlock special stages**
- 👁️ **Unlock secret bosses**
- 🎁 **Gain bonus items**
- 🏆 **Unlock different endings**

**💡 Tip**: No wrong choices! All paths have rewards.

---

## 🎬 Cutscenes

### What Are Cutscenes?
Cinematic story sequences that play before major battles:
- Animated backgrounds
- Character dialogue
- Story progression
- Beautiful visuals

### Controls
- **Next ▶**: Advance to next scene
- **Skip ⏭**: Skip entire cutscene
- **Progress Dots**: Show your progress

**💡 Tip**: Watch cutscenes once for the story, skip on replays!

## 👁️ Secret Bosses

Click the 👁️ button to view hidden bosses.

### 5 Secret Bosses to Find

#### 👹 Corrupted Guardian
- **How to Unlock**: Keep the artifact in Chapter 3
- **Reward**: +5 to all Dark cards
- **Difficulty**: SECRET

#### 👻 Spirit Elder
- **How to Unlock**: Destroy the artifact in Chapter 3
- **Reward**: Revive one card per match
- **Difficulty**: SECRET

#### 🌈 Elemental Fusion
- **How to Unlock**: Complete all chapters on Master
- **Reward**: Prismatic Deck (all elements)
- **Difficulty**: SECRET

#### ⏰ Time Keeper
- **How to Unlock**: Win 10 battles with 0 cards remaining
- **Reward**: Draw extra card every 3 turns
- **Difficulty**: SECRET

#### 🪞 Mirror Self
- **How to Unlock**: Complete game with all 3 endings
- **Reward**: Copy opponent's last card
- **Difficulty**: SECRET

**💡 Tip**: Secret bosses are much harder than regular stages!

## 📊 Story Progression Tips

### For Beginners
1. Start on **Warrior** difficulty
2. Complete Chapter 1 first
3. Read the backstories
4. Don't worry about choices - all are good!

### For Completionists
1. Play through on **Master** difficulty
2. Make different choices each playthrough
3. Unlock all secret bosses
4. Collect all endings
5. Read all backstories

### For Speedrunners
1. Use **Novice** difficulty
2. Skip cutscenes
3. Focus on main path
4. Save secret bosses for later

## 💬 Story Mode FAQs

**Q: Can I change my choices?**
A: Choices are permanent in a playthrough, but you can replay to make different choices!

**Q: Do I lose progress if I change difficulty?**
A: No! Completed stages stay complete. Change difficulty anytime.

**Q: How do I unlock secret bosses?**
A: Each has specific unlock conditions. Check the 👁️ panel for hints!

**Q: Can I replay stages?**
A: Yes! Click any completed stage to replay it.

**Q: What happens if I lose?**
A: You can retry immediately. No progress lost!

**Q: Are cutscenes required?**
A: No! You can skip them, but they add story depth.

**Q: How many endings are there?**
A: 3 main endings based on your final choice.

**Q: Do choices affect difficulty?**
A: No, but they unlock different content and rewards.

**Q: Can I see all backstories without fighting?**
A: No, you must defeat each character to unlock their backstory.

**Q: What's the hardest challenge?**
A: Legendary difficulty secret bosses!
