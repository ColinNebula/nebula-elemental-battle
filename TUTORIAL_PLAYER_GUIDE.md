# 🎓 Tutorial Mode - Player Guide

## What is Tutorial Mode?

Tutorial Mode is an interactive learning experience that teaches you how to play Elemental Battle by guiding you through your first complete battle. Instead of just reading instructions, you'll learn by actually playing the game with step-by-step guidance!

## How to Start Tutorial Mode

1. Launch **Elemental Battle**
2. On the Main Menu, click **GAMEPLAY**
3. Click **🎓 TUTORIAL MODE** (the green glowing button)
4. Follow the on-screen instructions!

## What You'll Learn

### Phase 1: Character Selection
- Choose your warrior avatar
- See their unique backstory
- Start your journey!

### Phase 2: Building Your Deck
- Receive 10 random cards
- Learn about card elements and strength
- Select your best 5 cards for battle
- Remaining 5 go to your reserve deck

### Phase 3: Battle Basics
- **Playing Cards**: Click cards to play them
- **Card Strength**: Higher strength wins the round
- **Scoring**: Win points equal to strength difference
- **Turn Order**: Alternate turns with your opponent

### Phase 4: Advanced Mechanics
- **Element Matching**: Play 2 same-element cards for DOUBLE STRENGTH! 🎯
- **Special Abilities**: 
  - 🌍 Earth: Draw from reserve deck
  - ⭐ Power: Auto-play another Power card
  - 🔥 Fire: Burn opponent's card
- **Mana System**: Cards cost mana to play (regenerates each turn)

### Phase 5: Power-Up Systems
- **💪 Boosters**: Temporary buffs for your cards
- **⚡ Ultimate Abilities**: Powerful cooldown-based attacks
- **⚔️ Equipment**: Permanent stat bonuses
- Located in the right sidebar!

### Phase 6: Strategic Features
- **Fusion**: Combine 2 cards for massive power
- **Trap**: Set traps for your opponent
- Buttons near your hand

### Phase 7: Free Play
- Complete the remaining rounds on your own
- Use everything you've learned!
- Try to win your first battle!

## Tutorial Features

### 🌟 Helpful Overlays
During the tutorial, you'll see instruction screens that explain what to do next. These screens:
- Have a **green border** (tutorial theme)
- Show your **progress dots** at the bottom
- Can be **skipped** using the ✕ button (top-right)

### 📍 Progress Tracking
The dots at the bottom of instruction screens show:
- **Gray dots**: Steps you haven't reached yet
- **Green glowing dot**: Current step (YOU ARE HERE!)
- **Semi-green dots**: Steps you've completed

### ⏭️ Skip Tutorial
Not needed? Click the **"Skip Tutorial ✕"** button in the top-right corner of any instruction screen to return to the main menu.

## Tips for Success

### 🎯 Take Your Time
- Read each instruction carefully
- There's no time limit during tutorial phases
- Experiment and learn!

### 🔄 Practice Makes Perfect
- Complete the full tutorial battle
- You can always play again in Quick Play or Story Mode
- Each battle teaches you something new

### 📖 Reference Guide
After completing the tutorial, you can always review the basics by clicking **HOW TO PLAY** in the main menu.

## What Happens After Tutorial?

### ✅ Tutorial Completed
When you finish the tutorial (win or lose), you'll:
1. See a completion message
2. Be marked as having completed the tutorial
3. Return to the Main Menu
4. Be ready to play on your own!

### 🚀 Ready for More?
After the tutorial, try these modes:
- **Quick Play**: Single battles with customizable difficulty
- **Story Mode**: Epic campaign with narrative battles
- **Inventory**: Unlock and equip power-ups
- **Theme Shop**: Customize your game appearance

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I replay the tutorial?
**A:** Yes! Just click Tutorial Mode from the main menu again. Your completion status doesn't prevent replaying.

### Q: What if I lose the tutorial battle?
**A:** That's okay! Losing is part of learning. You'll still complete the tutorial and understand the mechanics. You can always try Quick Play next!

### Q: Do I get rewards for completing the tutorial?
**A:** Currently, the reward is knowledge and confidence! Future updates may add tutorial completion rewards.

### Q: Can I skip specific tutorial steps?
**A:** No, but you can skip the entire tutorial using the ✕ button. Steps progress automatically based on your actions.

### Q: How long does the tutorial take?
**A:** Approximately 5-10 minutes, depending on your pace. It's a full battle with guidance!

### Q: Is the tutorial mandatory?
**A:** No! You can skip it and jump right into Quick Play or Story Mode if you prefer to learn by doing.
